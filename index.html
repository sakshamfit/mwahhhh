Does it actually not transfer?

Once it sets (60 seconds, tops) it stays put through coffee, cocktails and kisses. Melts off with any oil cleanser.

100%. No animal-derived ingredients, never tested on animals, and we'd never ask a bunny to wear lipstick.

Squalane, shea butter and hyaluronic acid keep it cushy for the full 12 hours. No flaking, no cracking, no crumbs.

Yes. The case is forever; bullets twist in and out. Refills are $16 and ship plastic-free.
